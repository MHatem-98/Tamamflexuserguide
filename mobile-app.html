Employee mobile app

Your Tamam Flex workday, in one app.

This guide explains how employees sign in, register attendance, check work information, follow tasks, and manage account preferences.

Start the guideDesktop guide

Before you begin

  1. Use the email address provided by your company.
  2. Allow location access when recording attendance, if asked.
  3. Keep your device secure and do not share your password.
What you see may vary. Available schedules, tasks, leave options, and settings are determined by your company and assigned role.

Step 01

Sign in and verify your account

Enter your email address and password, then complete the verification code sent to your email when prompted.

1

Enter your credentials

Type your company email and password. Select Remember me only on a personal, secure device. Use Forgot password? if you need to reset it.

2

Confirm the code

Enter the verification code sent to your email, then select Confirm code. Request another code only after the countdown ends.

Step 02

Check in and out

Your home screen shows the current shift, assigned group, location, and today’s attendance status.

1

Review today’s shift

Open the attendance prompt when your shift starts. Confirm the displayed group, location, and work period are correct.

2

Register attendance

Check that the location status shows you are within the allowed work area, then tap the fingerprint button to check in.

3

Check out when finished

After a successful check-in, the timer is displayed. Tap the red fingerprint button to register your departure at the end of the shift.

Attendance tip

If your location is not accepted, move to the assigned work location and try again. Contact your supervisor if the shift, location, or group information is incorrect.

Step 03

Follow your work

Use the bottom navigation to review attendance records, submit or view leave requests, and keep up with assigned tasks.

1

View attendance history

Choose Day, Week, or Period to review check-in, check-out, and total hours for the selected time range.

2

Manage leave requests

Open Leave to see active requests and their status. Use the plus button to start a new request when this option is available.

3

Find your tasks

Use the date, search, and status filters to find work. Open a task to review its dates, progress, and current status.

4

Review task details

Check the parent task, group, scheduled dates, progress, and delivery status. A completed or expired task cannot be changed.

Step 04

Manage your account

Open More to access your profile, notifications, password, language, appearance, help, and sign-out options.

1

Keep your account current

Use Profile to review your details, Notifications for alerts, and Change password to update your sign-in password. Select Sign out when using a shared device.
